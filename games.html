Five small games for a short break

Hand-built in vanilla JavaScript: no engines, no downloads. Your best scores stay on this device.

Dino Jump

The offline-browser classic, redrawn: run as far as you can, jumping cacti and ducking under birds.

How to play

  • Space or ↑ to jump. Hold it for a higher jump; tap for a short hop.
  • ↓ to duck under birds, or to drop faster mid-air.
  • On a phone, tap to jump and swipe down to duck.
  • It speeds up as you run. A chime marks every 100 points.

Stack 2048

Slide tiles together to merge them up the stack, from HTML all the way to Senior.

How to play

  • Arrow keys or WASD on a keyboard; swipe on the board on a phone.
  • Two equal tiles merge into the next step up. Reach Senior (2048) to win.
  • Your board is saved, so you can come back later.

    How to play

    • Tap the tower, or press Space, to drop the sliding block.
    • Whatever hangs over the edge gets trimmed, so the next block is smaller.
    • Land three perfect drops in a row and the block starts to grow back.

    Rickshaw Rush

    Rush hour in Dhaka. Weave your rickshaw through buses and CNGs, and grab cha on the way.

    Score

    How to play

    • Left and right arrow keys (or A and D) change lane. On a phone, swipe or tap either side.
    • Each cup of cha is worth 25 points; distance adds the rest.
    • Press Space or tap the bell to ring it. It doesn't help, but it's Dhaka.
